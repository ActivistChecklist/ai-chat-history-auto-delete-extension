Automatically delete your Claude chat history

Your old AI chats stay in your Claude account until you delete them. Anthropic could be forced to turn them over to the cops (or to the other side in a lawsuit), and a hacker who gets into your account could read them. This extension deletes chats you haven't updated in a set number of days (30 by default).

Add to Chrome

Get it from the Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons.

How it works

  1. Install the extension, then open claude.ai in a normal tab and sign in like you usually do.
  2. In the settings, choose how many days a chat can go without being updated before it gets deleted (30 by default). You can have it run once a day, once a week, once a month, or only when you click Run now. By default, it skips starred chats and asks you to approve the list before deleting anything. (You can turn either of those off in the settings, but Run now always asks first.)
  3. The extension only runs while you have claude.ai open in a tab, because it needs your login there to delete chats. If you haven't had claude.ai open for a while, the next check will find all the chats that passed your limit in the meantime.

Privacy

The extension runs in your browser and only talks to claude.ai. Nothing about you or your chats is sent to us, so we don't have anything to turn over if we get a legal demand. It saves your settings and how many chats were deleted on each run over the last 30 days (not which ones). It also keeps the list of chats waiting for your approval until you confirm the deletion or a later run replaces it. All of this stays in your browser.

If you have questions, you can read our privacy policy or email us at the address listed there. If you'd like, you can also review the code on GitHub.
